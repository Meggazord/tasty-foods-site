Olives FAQ

Which olive varieties and cuts do you offer?+

Green and black olives whole, pitted or sliced, plus whole Kalamata olives.

Do your olives contain added oil or preservatives?+

No. Our olives have no added oil, no preservatives and no artificial colors or flavors. They are vegan and gluten free.

Do you offer food-service sizes?+

Yes. All our olives can be supplied in food-service formats, alongside retail jars from 7.05 oz (200 g) to 31.75 oz (900 g).
