Jam FAQ

What's the difference between jam, fruit spread and fruit preserve?+

Fruit content and texture. Fruit Spread is our lighter 25% fruit line, Jam is 45% fruit with a classic set, and Fruit Preserve is our premium 45% fruit line with chunkier fruit in a premium jar.

Is your jam made with high-fructose corn syrup?+

No. All three lines are made with real fruit and no high-fructose corn syrup, and are gluten free.

Is your jam available for food service?+

Yes. All three lines can be supplied in food-service formats for restaurants, hotels, bakeries and caterers.
