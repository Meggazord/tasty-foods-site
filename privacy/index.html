Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Tasty Foods Global LLC (“we”, “us”) operates tastyfoods.global. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you enter in the form: your name, company, work email, phone number (if provided), business type, the products and quantities you're interested in, whether you will pick up or need delivery, and your delivery ZIP code.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send your quotation and follow up on your request.
  • To protect the website and our team from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing mailing lists.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with service providers who help us run this website and handle enquiries (for example hosting and email providers), and only as needed for those purposes, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us. Send your request through the quote form and choose “Other” as your business type.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
