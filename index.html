Buyer FAQ

Do you sell wholesale only?+

Yes. We supply U.S. retailers, distributors and food-service buyers with our own brands, Nadia's Orchard and Tasty Twist.

Where are your products made, and how are they certified?+

In internationally certified facilities in Egypt — FSSC 22000, BRCGS, ISO 22000, HACCP, Halal, Kosher and U.S. FDA facility registration. Certifications vary by category and SKU.

What shelf life do your products have?+

Jams, juices and pasta: 2 years. Condiments: 6–15 months and dressings: 6–18 months, depending on SKU.

What is your minimum order?+

It varies by product and pack size. Send us your request through the quote form and we’ll confirm the minimum order for each item.

Can I get samples before ordering?+

Yes — request samples with the form below and tell us which SKUs you're evaluating.
